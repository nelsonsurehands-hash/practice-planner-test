Questions coaches ask

Is the basketball play designer free?

Yes. You can try it on this page without an account, and creating a team in The Extra Coach to save your plays is free.

Do I need to download an app?

No. It runs in the browser on iPhone, iPad, Android, Chromebook, Mac and PC. You can also add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

Can my plays animate?

Yes. Every step plays back with players moving, the ball passing and defenders reacting, so players can see the timing, not just the lines.

Can I show the play against a defense?

Yes. Turn on man-to-man, 2-3 zone, 3-2 zone or box-and-1, and the defenders move with the ball automatically.

Can I share plays with my players?

Yes. Send one link and players can step through or watch every play on their phone without signing up. You can also print plays as a PDF.

Can I bring in the plays I already have?

Yes. Take a photo of a play on a whiteboard, in a notebook or on a printout and the app draws it for you, one photo per step. You can also import a backup file of plays and drills from another coach.

Does it work for other sports?

Yes. Besides basketball, there are boards for volleyball, soccer, football and baseball or softball.
