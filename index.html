What it does

Everything off your clipboard

⏱️

Timed practice plans

Build practice from your drill library, see every block on the clock, and print a copy for the gym.

🏀

Plays that move

Draw a play once and watch it run step by step. Add man, 2-3, 3-2 or box-and-1 defense automatically.

📅

One season calendar

Practices, games, days off and bus times in one place, with a single link parents can always open.

🔴

Live stat tracker

Tap shots, rebounds and assists during the game. Season totals add themselves up.

🚌

Parent admin

Bus list check-in, game-day food sign-ups and a schedule page parents can read without an account.

📸

Snap a photo to import

Take a picture of a paper schedule or whiteboard play and the app reads it in for you.

Bring what you already have

Don't start over. Take a picture.

Your plays are on a whiteboard, practice plans are in a binder, the schedule is a PDF from the district, and the roster is on a sign-up sheet. Snap a photo and the app turns it into the real thing. You check it before anything is saved.

🏀

Plays from a whiteboard

Photograph a play from the whiteboard, your notebook or a printout. It comes in as an animated play you can edit. Drawn across several pictures? Add one photo per step.

⏱️

Practice plans from paper

Snap last season's practice sheet or today's whiteboard. Every drill comes in timed and in order, and new drills go straight into your library.

📅

Schedules from a photo

A game schedule, district calendar or screenshot becomes games and dates on your calendar, up to four pages at once.

📋

Rosters in seconds

Snap a printed or handwritten roster, or paste a list straight from a spreadsheet. Names, numbers and parent contacts fill in when they’re on the sheet.

🔁

Drills and plays from another coach

Coaches can share a backup file with their drills, plays and practice plans. Import it and pick what you want to keep.

🎨

Your logo, your colors

Snap your team logo and the app pulls the team colors from it, so every page and parent link looks like your program.

Playbook

Teach it the way it looks on the floor

Every step of a play animates with notes underneath, so players see the cut, the screen and the pass. Share the playbook with players by link.

Calendar

Parents stop asking "what time is practice?"

Change a practice time once and every coach and parent sees it. Parents can add the whole season to their phone's calendar.

Getting started

Up and running in five minutes

  1. Create your teamPick your sport, colors and the features you want. Snap your logo and the app pulls the team colors from it. Then snap your schedule, roster, plays and practice plans to bring them in.
  2. Bring your staffSend an invite link. Assistant coaches tap it and join with exactly the access you choose, across one team or every team at your school.
  3. Send parents one linkA short link to the schedule that's always up to date. No account, no app to download.
