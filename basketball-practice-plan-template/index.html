Practice planning questions

How long should a basketball practice be?

For youth teams, 60 to 75 minutes is plenty. Middle school practices usually run 90 minutes, and high school teams often go 2 hours. Shorter, well-planned practices beat long ones where players stand around.

How should I structure a basketball practice?

Warm up, then individual skills (ball handling, finishing, shooting), then team defense, then team offense, then a scrimmage or game situations. End with free throws and a short team talk. Put water breaks between hard blocks.

How many drills should be in a practice?

Usually 8 to 12 blocks. Keep each drill between 5 and 15 minutes so players stay sharp, and repeat the same core drills each week so practice runs itself.
