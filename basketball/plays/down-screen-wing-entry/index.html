Down Screen Wing Entry

When the wing is being denied, a down screen is the cleanest way to get the ball there. A player sets a screen for a teammate on the block, who curls or pops to the wing. The screener then slips to the basket. It works as a play on its own or as the first action of almost any offense.

How to run it

  1. 2 starts on the right block. 4 comes down and sets a down screen on 2's defender.
  2. 2 sets up her defender low, then comes off the screen to the wing. 1 makes the entry pass while 4 slips to the rim.
  3. 2 catches ready to shoot. If her defender trails the screen, it's an open jumper.

Coaching points

  • The cutter walks her defender into the screen before she comes off it.
  • The screener's back faces where the cutter wants to go.
  • Read the defender: trail = curl to the middle, cheat over the top = pop or flare.
  • The screener slips as soon as the cutter clears; that's when the defense is out of position.

Reads

  • Defender trails the screen: curl toward the middle for a layup or short jumper.
  • Defender goes under: pop to the wing for the shot.
  • Defenders switch: hit the screener slipping to the rim.
