3-Man Weave

The 3-man weave is a classic for a reason. It teaches passing on the move, filling lanes and finishing at full speed, all without dribbling. Three players start at half court: pass to a teammate, run behind her, and keep the weave going until someone finishes the layup.

How the drill works

  1. Three lanes at half court. The middle player (1) passes to 2 on the right.
  2. 1 runs behind 2 to fill the right lane. 2 moves to the middle, 3 runs ahead on the left.
  3. 2 passes to 3 on the left and runs behind her.
  4. 3 moves to the middle, 2 fills the left lane, 1 runs ahead on the right.
  5. 3 passes ahead to 1. Lead pass, no dribble.
  6. 1 attacks the rim for the layup. 2 and 3 crash for the rebound.

Coaching points

  • No dribbles: the ball moves only by passing.
  • Lead passes, chest high, in front of the receiver.
  • Always pass and go behind the person you passed to.
  • Sprint the lanes wide, then finish with two hands or a soft layup off the glass.

Variations

  • Add a 2-on-1 coming back the other way.
  • Make it 11-man break or a timed weave: down and back in under 20 seconds.
  • Finish with a pull-up jumper instead of a layup.
